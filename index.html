Our Offerings

Professional Music Services

Everything a singer or creator needs to go from a simple hum to a release-ready masterpiece.

Audio Recording

Professionally isolated recording booth equipped with top-tier condenser mics to capture every nuance of your vocals or instruments.

Music Production

Custom beat-making and arrangements. We create beats across CG Folk, Devotional Jasgeet, Bollywood, Rap, and Pop styles.

Mixing & Mastering

Balanced dynamics, pristine high-ends, and robust low-ends. Get your tracks volume-matched and formatted for Spotify, YouTube, and Apple Music.

Voice Over

Crystal clear voice recording for podcasts, audiobooks, local advertisements, commentary, documentary narration, and presentations.

Video Editing

Professional cinematic editing, color grading, multi-camera sync, transitions, and lyrical formatting for music video final cuts.

Song Shoot

Complete music video production. We manage storyboard, location scouting, professional camera crews, directing, and editing.
